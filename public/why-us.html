Transparency & Standards

Credentials Explained in Plain English

Every contractor claims to be "certified" and "the best." In an unregulated state like Texas, anyone with a ladder and a truck can call themselves a roofer. Here is exactly what our credentials mean and how we hold ourselves accountable.

RCAT Licensed Contractor

📜

Why it matters in Texas:

Texas is one of the few states with no mandatory state license for roofing contractors. Anyone can register an LLC overnight. The Roofing Contractors Association of Texas (RCAT) operates a rigorous voluntary licensing exam covering business ethics, insurance standards, and building codes. We voluntarily submit to this scrutiny.

GAF Master Elite Contractor

🏆

Held by fewer than 3% of North American roofers:

GAF is the largest shingle manufacturer in North America. Master Elite is its highest contractor certification, requiring ongoing installer training, continuous good standing, and verifiable insurance. It enables us to offer manufacturer-backed Golden Pledge warranties that protect you even if the contractor were ever to cease business.

Better Business Bureau (BBB) A+

🛡

The highest rating for complaint resolution:

BBB ratings evaluate transparent business practices, advertising accuracy, licensing compliance, and responsiveness to customer inquiries. An A+ rating reflects years of resolved inquiries and verified local accountability.

FORTIFIED Roof Standard

🌪

Engineered for severe Gulf Coast storms:

Developed by the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS), FORTIFIED roofing requires sealed roof decks with ring-shank nails, taped deck seams, and enhanced drip edge fastening to keep wind-driven water out of your home even if shingles blow off.

Warranty Realism: Read Past the "Lifetime" Headline

Many roofers wave around "50-Year" or "Lifetime" brochures without explaining how warranties actually work. Here are the 4 facts every property owner should know:

1. Material vs Workmanship

Manufacturer warranties cover shingle defects (extremely rare). Most leaks happen due to flashing errors or workmanship mistakes, which require separate workmanship warranties.

2. System Registration

A manufacturer warranty is only valid if the full component system (underlayment, starter strips, ridge vent) is installed to specification and officially registered with the factory.

3. Match Your Ownership Horizon

If you plan to sell in 3 years, paying for non-transferable 50-year warranty upgrades makes little financial sense. We align coverage with your actual plans.

4. Transferability Clauses

A good warranty transfers to the next buyer within a set window, adding measurable appraised value when you list your property on HAR.
