STATUTORY NOTICE REQUIRED BY TEX. BUS. & COM. CODE § 27.02

"Texas law requires a person insured under a property insurance policy to pay any deductible applicable to a claim made against the policy. It is a violation of Texas law for a seller of goods or services who reasonably expects to be paid wholly or partly from the proceeds of a property insurance claim to knowingly allow the insured person to fail to pay, or assist the insured person's failure to pay, the applicable insurance deductible."

1. Scope of Services & Relationship

Houston Roofing & Construction provides residential, commercial, and multi-family roofing inspections, repair services, and replacements. We operate strictly as roofing contractors and not as licensed public insurance adjusters.

2. Appointment Booking & Cancellation Policy

Appointments booked through our online automated system reserve dedicated staff capacity. Customers may reschedule or cancel appointments directly through the customer portal up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled appointment start time.

Manufacturer warranties and workmanship warranties are governed by explicit written project agreements provided following inspection and scope confirmation.
