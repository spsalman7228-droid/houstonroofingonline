Getting Started

How do I get an estimate or quote?

Book an inspection online. We inspect the whole roof system, document what we find with high-resolution photos, and walk you through tiered Good / Better / Best repair and replacement options.

Select your service, choose an open date and time slot with guaranteed capacity, fill in your property details, and confirm. Sign in with Google or your phone to track your appointment and follow progress.

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel directly from your customer portal up to 24 hours before your scheduled appointment time.

Process & Timeline

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family home replacements take 1 to 2 days on-site, depending on roof square footage, pitch complexity, and weather. Larger commercial or multi-family projects are scheduled in phased milestones.

How will I know what is happening during installation?

Your customer dashboard displays the live stage out of 11 standardized milestones, on-site photos uploaded by your in-house project manager, and a timestamped history of updates.

Storm Damage & Texas Insurance Laws

Do you handle or negotiate my insurance claim?

No. In Texas, a roofing contractor cannot act as a public adjuster while also performing the contracted work. We document storm damage with date-stamped photos and technical specifications for you to submit to your insurer. You manage and file your claim.

Will you cover, waive, or absorb my deductible?

No. Under Texas Business & Commerce Code § 27.02, it is a criminal offense for contractors to waive, rebate, or absorb a deductible. The property owner must pay the required deductible. Beware of contractors offering "free roofs."

How long does my insurance carrier have to respond?

Per Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) statutes, an insurer has 15 business days to acknowledge receipt of a claim, and once agreed, 5 business days to issue payment.

Warranties & Credentials

Does Texas require roofers to be licensed?

No. Texas does not issue a state license for roofing contractors. That is why we hold a voluntary license with the Roofing Contractors Association of Texas (RCAT) to demonstrate code compliance and insurance verification.

What is GAF Master Elite certification?

Master Elite is the highest certification level from GAF, the largest North American shingle maker. Only the top 2%–3% of contractors qualify, enabling us to register factory-backed Golden Pledge warranties.

Texas Insurance Law Disclosure: We are roofing contractors, not public adjusters. Deductibles cannot be waived or absorbed under Texas law.
