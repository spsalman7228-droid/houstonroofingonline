Commercial & Multi-Family

Low-Slope Engineering & Phased Community Installations

Managing commercial roofs in Houston requires balancing drainage ponding, heavy HVAC traffic, tenant operations, and hurricane wind codes. Our leadership brings 20+ years of commercial roofing oversight across Greater Houston.

Commercial Flat & Low-Slope Roof Systems

Single-Ply

TPO & PVC Membranes

Thermoplastic polyolefin (TPO) delivers high solar reflectance (reducing summer HVAC cooling costs) and heat-welded seams for puncture resistance on industrial and retail roofs.

  • Heat-reflective white membranes
  • Chemically resistant PVC options
  • Carlisle & Duro-Last systems
Multi-Layer

Modified Bitumen (SBS & APP)

Multi-ply asphalt systems reinforced with fiberglass or polyester mats. Superior puncture tolerance for roofs with frequent maintenance personnel and mechanical equipment servicing.

  • Heavy foot-traffic durability
  • Cold-applied or torch-grade options
  • Redundant multi-ply protection
Restoration

Elastomeric & Silicone Coatings

Extend existing commercial roof life by 10–15 years without the cost or disruption of a full tear-off. Seamless liquid-applied membranes seal seams and stop ponding water leaks.

  • Fully tax-deductible maintenance expense
  • Zero disruption to business operations
  • Seamless monolithic waterproof seal

Apartment & Multi-Family Replacement Protocol

Replacing roofs on occupied apartment complexes or HOA townhome communities requires tight logistics. We work directly with property managers to minimize tenant complaints:

1. Phased Staging

One building at a time from tear-off to dry-in. Dumpsters and material staging are restricted to designated stalls.

2. Resident Safety

Covered pedestrian entrance tunnels, daily magnetic sweeps for nails, and 48-hour advance parking notices.

3. Live Manager Portal

Property managers track milestone completion, photos, and inspection sign-offs online building by building.
