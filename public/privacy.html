Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Concept Policy

This privacy notice outlines how Houston Roofing & Construction ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through this website.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information necessary to coordinate on-site roof inspections, deliver diagnostic estimates, and communicate regarding scheduled projects. This includes your name, property address, contact phone number, email address, and optional project notes.

2. Contact Consent & Communications

We do not sell, rent, or lease customer contact lists to third-party telemarketers. When you submit a booking, you provide consent for our team to contact you via phone or in-app message regarding your requested appointment. We maintain a secure record of this consent with a timestamp.

3. Data Storage & Security

Customer records are stored securely using Firebase Firestore with strict access control rules. Customers may only access their own project documents and appointments.

4. Legal Disclosures

This website is an unsolicited concept redesign and is not the official website of the operating company. All customer reviews shown as concept samples are explicitly marked as such.
