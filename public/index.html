Tiered Transparency

Good / Better / Best Options

Every proposal includes clear tiered options so you select the exact balance of warranty coverage, storm resilience, and budget that fits how long you plan to own the property.

Tier 1

Good: Dependable Shingles

A cost-conscious, code-compliant shingle system built for reliable protection against Houston heat.

Best For: Budget-focused repairs or properties planned for near-term resale.
  • Standard 3-tab / architectural shingles
  • Standard synthetic underlayment
  • Code-compliant ridge ventilation
Most Popular
Tier 2

Better: Upgraded Architectural

Thicker, impact-resistant architectural shingles and enhanced leak barriers across all valleys and eaves.

Best For: Most Houston homeowners wanting balanced long-term durability and storm peace of mind.
  • Heavyweight architectural laminate
  • Ice & water shield in critical leak valleys
  • High-performance ridge vent system
  • GAF System Plus eligible warranty
Tier 3

Best: Standing Seam Metal

Concealed fastener metal roofing engineered for maximum wind uplift ratings and lifetime longevity.

Best For: Property owners planning to stay long-term who want maximum storm resilience.
  • 24-gauge standing seam metal panels
  • High-temperature underlayment barrier
  • Highest radiant heat reflectance
  • Class 4 impact resistance rating
