Residential Expertise

Houston Home Roofing: Honest Options for Texas Weather

Houston heat, high humidity, windstorms, and sudden hail can punish residential roofs. We don't push one-size-fits-all replacements. We inspect, show you high-resolution photos, and help you decide between a targeted repair and a full replacement.

Repair or Replace: How We Help You Decide

Too many contractors declare every roof "totaled" to sell a full job. Here is our plain-English framework:

When a Repair Makes Sense

  • Isolated leaks caused by pipe boot or flashing failures
  • Shingles are supple with intact granules (<12 years old)
  • Wind damage limited to a single roof facet
  • Attic decking shows no widespread structural rot

When Replacement is Better Long-Term

  • Widespread granule loss and brittle shingle fiberglass exposure
  • Extensive hail impacts bruising multiple slopes
  • Repeated chronic leaks across multiple rooms
  • Roof is over 18–20 years old and insurer flagged it for renewal

Asphalt Shingle vs. Standing Seam Metal

Asphalt Shingle Systems

The most popular residential roof in Houston. High aesthetic versatility with architectural profile lines, excellent wind ratings up to 130 mph, and affordable upfront capital.

Typical Lifespan in Houston: 18–25 years depending on attic ventilation and tree cover.

Standing Seam Metal Systems

Engineered panels with concealed fasteners. High solar reflectance cuts heat absorption in Houston's blazing summers, and interlocking seams resist high gale wind uplift.

Typical Lifespan in Houston: 50+ years with virtually zero maintenance.
